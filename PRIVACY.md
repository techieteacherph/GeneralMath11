## 2. What we collect

| Information | Where it comes from | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Student Code** (class number + section + last name) | Typed by the learner | To identify the learner in the class |
| **Name** as shown in the app: first and last name from the teacher's roster, or last name only on devices that use the public roster file | Teacher's roster | To show the learner's name and label results |
| **Section** and **class number** | Teacher's roster | To group learners by class |
| **Gender** (if included in the roster file) | Teacher's roster | Roster records and progress exports. Not shown to learners and not published. |
| **Nickname** (optional) | Chosen by the learner | To appear on the leaderboard instead of a real name |
| **Practice progress, XP and level** | Learner's activity | To show progress and motivate learners |
| **Check-up results** (lesson, score out of 5, date and time) | Learner's activity | So the teacher can see learning progress |
| Check-up **answers** | Learner's activity | Kept **only on the learner's device**; not sent to the teacher's Drive |

We do **not** collect addresses, phone numbers, email addresses, photos, location, government ID numbers, health or financial information, and we do not use advertising or analytics tools.

## 3. Why we use it

- To let learners practise and take check-ups.
- To let the teacher see class progress and results for the subject.
- To keep the leaderboard running (nicknames and XP only).
- To keep the app working (settings, offline copies, queued results).

We do not sell personal information or use it for marketing or profiling.

## 4. Lawful basis and consent

The app is used for the school's legitimate educational purposes. Because learners are minors, the school should obtain **parent or guardian consent** (or rely on the consent already given at enrolment, where it covers this use). By continuing to use the app, the learner confirms they have read this notice. A parent or guardian may withdraw consent at any time (see section 9).

## 5. Where information is stored and who receives it

**On the learner's device (browser storage).** Name, Student Code, progress, XP, nickname, check-up answers, queued results and any roster saved on that device. This stays on the device until the learner or teacher clears the browser's site data.

**Teacher's Google Drive folder.** When a check-up is submitted, a small result file is saved: nickname, XP, Student Code, name, section, lesson, score, total and time. Only the teacher (and anyone the teacher authorizes) can open this folder.

**Public roster file (optional).** If the teacher publishes `roster.json`, anyone who knows the website address can see each learner's **section, class number and last name** (no first name or gender). The teacher decides whether to publish it.

**Service providers.** The app relies on these services, which may process technical data such as IP address and device type as part of normal operation:

| Service | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Cloudflare | Hosts the website and domain |
| GitHub | Stores the app's files |
| Google (Sheets, Drive, Apps Script) | Lesson sheet, files and results storage |
| YouTube (privacy-enhanced mode) | Lesson videos, loaded only when the learner taps *Watch here* |

These providers may store data outside the Philippines under their own terms and privacy policies. The app does not use cookies for tracking; it uses the browser's local storage only to remember settings and progress.

**Who sees what**

- Other learners: only nicknames and XP on the leaderboard.
- The teacher: names, codes, sections and results.
- School administrators or DepEd: only as required by school policy or law.

## 7. How we protect it

- Data is minimal and mostly stays on the learner's device.
- The website is delivered over HTTPS.
- Result files go to a private Drive folder.
- The teacher's Admin panel is protected by an access code. This code is a basic lock and **not** strong security (see the technical documentation, *Known limitations*), so please do not share it and lock the panel after use.
- Teachers should keep their Google and GitHub accounts secured with strong passwords and two-step verification.

No system is perfectly secure. If a personal data breach affecting learners occurs, the school will respond and notify affected persons and the National Privacy Commission as required by law.

## 8. Children's privacy

The app is for Grade 11 learners. Learners should never put contact details, home addresses or other personal information into the nickname or any text box. Nicknames should not be real names.

## 9. Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act you have the right to:

1. **be informed** about how your data is used (this notice);
2. **access** the data we hold about you;
3. **object** to processing and withdraw consent;
4. **correct** inaccurate data;
5. **erase or block** data that is no longer needed or was unlawfully processed;
6. **data portability** (get your data in a commonly used format);
7. **claim damages** if you were harmed by inaccurate, incomplete, outdated, false, unlawfully obtained or unauthorized use of your data;
8. **file a complaint** with the **National Privacy Commission** (privacy.gov.ph).

To use these rights, contact the teacher or Data Protection Officer in section 1. A parent or guardian may act for a learner who is a minor.

**Clearing your data on a device:** in the browser's settings, clear site data for this website (or remove the installed app). Ask the teacher to delete any result files held in Drive.
